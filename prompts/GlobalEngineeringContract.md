# Global Engineering Contract

- Inspect the repository and current tests before editing.
- Execute exactly one active unit and stop at its boundary.
- Preserve working behavior, completed history, decisions, issues, and evidence.
- Keep stable logic in `src/`; notebooks explain and orchestrate.
- Use missing values honestly; never substitute zero without a defined meaning.
- Separate platforms when public metrics are not comparable.
- Keep full transcripts, private/raw data, credentials, and unauthorized personal information out of Git.
- Treat AI suggestions as candidates; require source evidence or deterministic verification.
- Save actual test output and manual evidence under `evidence/<UnitID>/`.
- Never fabricate data, results, citations, commits, tests, or human review.
- Never describe association as causation or one viewer as a population.
